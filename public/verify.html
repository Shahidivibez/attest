What this does and does not establish. The Groth16 check runs locally against the verification key served with the proof, so it proves the proof is valid for that key. It does not by itself prove the key is the one we should be using — that is what registering the key on zkVerify establishes, and it is why anchoring matters. The hash-chain check is recomputed here from the entries, using your browser's own SHA-256.
